**Effective date:** August 25, 2026
{: .notice--info}

This Privacy Policy describes how Nathaniel Knudsen ("I", "me", "my") handles
information in connection with the software applications I publish and this
website, [stoicswe.com](https://www.stoicswe.com).

The short version: **my applications do not collect your personal data.** There
are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The only
information I ever receive is what you choose to send me directly when you
contact me about a problem.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to:

- **The applications I write and publish** — referred to below as "the Apps".
- **This website**, stoicswe.com.

It does not apply to software I write in the course of my employment, to
third-party services you may reach through links on this site, or to any
platform through which you obtain the Apps.

## 2. Information the Apps Collect

**None.**

The Apps do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data. In
particular, the Apps do not:

- require you to create an account or provide a name, email address, or phone number;
- include analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting, attribution, or advertising SDKs;
- collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location data;
- build a profile of you or track you across applications or websites;
- transmit your content to any server operated by me.

I do not operate application servers, and I do not maintain a database of
users. There is no user data in my possession to disclose, sell, or lose.

## 3. Where Your App Data Is Stored

Content you create in the Apps is stored **on your own device** and, where the
App offers synchronization, in **your personal iCloud account**.

This data is written to your private iCloud database. It belongs to you and
resides within Apple's infrastructure, under your Apple Account. **I have no
access to it.** I cannot read, retrieve, export, or delete it, and I receive no
notification of its contents.

Because this data is held by Apple on your behalf, its storage, security,
encryption, retention, and availability are governed by Apple's terms and
policies rather than by me — specifically the
[iCloud Terms of Service](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/)
and the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). I
encourage you to review both.

To manage or delete this data, use the App's own controls, or manage your
iCloud storage directly through your device settings or
[iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com). Deleting the App's data from iCloud
removes it; I retain no copy.

## 4. Information You Provide When You Contact Me

The single exception to Section 2 is correspondence you initiate.

If you email me or message me on Bluesky about a bug, a question, or a feature
request, I receive whatever you choose to include — typically your email
address or handle, a description of the issue, and sometimes screenshots,
diagnostic output, or log excerpts that you attach voluntarily.

- This information is provided **entirely at your discretion**. Nothing is collected automatically.
- I use it **solely to understand and resolve the issue you raised**, or to answer your question.
- I do **not** sell, rent, share, or transfer it, and I do not use it for marketing.
- Correspondence is retained only as long as needed to resolve the matter and keep a reasonable record of reported issues, and may be deleted on request.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, government identifiers, health
information, or other sensitive personal data. I do not need it to help you,
and email is not a secure channel.

## 5. This Website

stoicswe.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It **sets no cookies and
runs no analytics**, and I collect no personal information through it.

Two things are nonetheless worth stating plainly:

**Hosting.** As with any website, the host — GitHub, Inc. — may log technical
request data such as IP addresses for security and operational purposes. This
is handled under
[GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

**Third-party resources.** Some pages load resources directly from third
parties. When they do, your browser contacts those services and they can see
your IP address and request. These are:

| Service | Where | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Fonts | All pages | Web typefaces |
| GitHub API | Projects page | Lists my public repositories |
| jsDelivr and Bluesky | Bluesky page | Renders the embedded feed |
| YouTube (`youtube-nocookie`) | Music player | Embedded playback |
| ipify | Network pane, on open | Shows you your own IP address |

No personal information is sent to these services by me; the requests are made
by your browser as part of loading the page. If you would rather they not be
made, a content blocker will stop them without breaking the rest of the site.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Apps and this website are not directed at children, and I do not knowingly
collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## 7. Your Rights

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is generally nothing to
access, correct, port, or erase. Where you have contacted me directly, you may
ask me to delete our correspondence, and I will do so unless I am required to
keep it. Data held in your iCloud account is under your own control and subject
to Apple's policies as described in Section 3.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, I will update it on this page and revise the effective
date above. Material changes will be described in the site's release notes or
commit history, which is public.

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in my Apps generally, are
